🏫 About AKGEC

Ajay Kumar Garg Engineering College (AKGEC) Ghaziabad is one of the premier institutes affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow. The college is known for its excellent academic environment, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and focus on research & innovation.

💻 Department of Computer Science and Engineering

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) at AKGEC is one of the largest departments offering undergraduate (B.Tech), postgraduate (M.Tech), and research programs in Computer Science.

🔹 Key Features:

  • Highly qualified and experienced faculty members.
  • Well-equipped computer labs with latest hardware and software.
  • Focus on cutting-edge technologies like Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cyber Security, Data Science, and Cloud Computing.
  • Active participation in Hackathons, Coding Competitions, and Research Projects.

📘 Courses Offered:

  1. B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering
  2. B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI/ML)
  3. B.Tech in Data Science
  4. M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering
